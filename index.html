About Me

I'm a DevOps Engineer at Mapal Group, working fully remote from Madrid. I got here the practical way: I joined Simple KYC as an intern in 2024, was promoted to full-time DevOps a year later, and spent that time on a small team that owned all of the infrastructure and IT. That meant the Azure environment, the pipelines, the Linux servers in production, and the incident response when something broke.

The work I value most is the kind nobody notices. I migrated PostgreSQL and MySQL databases from Azure Single Server to Flexible Server without a single incident, defined infrastructure in Terraform, and shipped through Jenkins, Docker, Kubernetes and ArgoCD. I treat security as part of the job rather than a final step, and I'd rather automate a task once than do it carefully twice.
